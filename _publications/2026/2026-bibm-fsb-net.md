abstract: >-
  A frequency-spatial fusion network for brain stroke lesion segmentation in non-contrast CT. FSB-Net adds a
  Wavelet Boundary Detection Head (WBDH) that decomposes encoder features into low-frequency approximation and
  directional high-frequency detail, a Frequency-Spatial Cross-Attention module (FSCAM) for bidirectional
  interaction between boundary and spatial features, and a Spectral Boundary Loss (SBL) that penalizes
  high-frequency differences from the ground truth in the Fourier domain, together yielding sharper lesion
  boundaries than spatial-only decoders.
